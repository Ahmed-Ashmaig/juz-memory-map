# Similars brief: Surah 45 Al-Jathiyah

Each group lists every occurrence in muṣḥaf order. "DIFFERS" lists the words that differ from the first occurrence in this surah.

## g1: matches ayat in other surahs · 4 occurrences

### 39:1: Az-Zumar, page 458, same words
- Before (38:88): And you will surely know [the truth of] its information after a time."
- THE AYAH: تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ | The revelation of the Book [i.e., the Qur’ān] is from Allāh, the Exalted in Might, the Wise.
- After (39:2): Indeed, We have sent down to you the Book, [O Muḥammad], in truth. So worship Allāh, [being] sincere to Him in religion.

### 40:2: Ghafir, page 467, words differ
- Before (40:1): Ḥā, Meem.
- THE AYAH: تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡعَلِيمِ | The revelation of the Book [i.e., the Qur’ān] is from Allāh, the Exalted in Might, the Knowing,
- DIFFERS: ٱلۡعَلِيمِ
- After (40:3): The forgiver of sin, acceptor of repentance, severe in punishment, owner of abundance. There is no deity except Him; to Him is the destination.

### 45:2: Al-Jathiyah, page 499, same words
- Before (45:1): Ḥā, Meem.
- THE AYAH: تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ | The revelation of the Book is from Allāh, the Exalted in Might, the Wise.
- After (45:3): Indeed, within the heavens and earth are signs for the believers.

### 46:2: Al-Ahqaf, page 502, same words
- Before (46:1): Ḥā, Meem.
- THE AYAH: تَنزِيلُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبِ مِنَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلۡعَزِيزِ ٱلۡحَكِيمِ | The revelation of the Book is from Allāh, the Exalted in Might, the Wise.
- After (46:3): We did not create the heavens and earth and what is between them except in truth and [for] a specified term. But those who disbelieve, from that of which they are warned, are turning away.

## g2: matches ayat in other surahs · 3 occurrences

### 16:34: An-Nahl, page 270, words differ
- Before (16:33): Do they [i.e., the disbelievers] await except that the angels should come to them or there comes the command of your Lord? Thus did those do before them. And Allāh wronged them not, but they had been wronging themselves.
- THE AYAH: فَأَصَابَهُمۡ سَيِّـَٔاتُ مَا عَمِلُواْ وَحَاقَ بِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ | So they were struck by the evil consequences of what they did and were enveloped by what they used to ridicule.
- DIFFERS: فَأَصَابَهُمۡ
- After (16:35): And those who associate others with Allāh say, "If Allāh had willed, we would not have worshipped anything other than Him, neither we nor our fathers, nor would we have forbidden anything through other than Him." Thus did those do before them. So is there upon the messengers except [the duty of] clear notification?

### 39:48: Az-Zumar, page 464, words differ
- Before (39:47): And if those who did wrong had all that is in the earth entirely and the like of it with it, they would [attempt to] ransom themselves thereby from the worst of the punishment on the Day of Resurrection. And there will appear to them from Allāh that which they had not taken into account.
- THE AYAH: وَبَدَا لَهُمۡ سَيِّـَٔاتُ مَا كَسَبُواْ وَحَاقَ بِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ | And there will appear to them the evils they had earned, and they will be enveloped by what they used to ridicule.
- DIFFERS: كَسَبُواْ
- After (39:49): And when adversity touches man, he calls upon Us; then when We bestow on him a favor from Us, he says, "I have only been given it because of [my] knowledge." Rather, it is a trial, but most of them do not know.

### 45:33: Al-Jathiyah, page 502, same words
- Before (45:32): And when it was said, 'Indeed, the promise of Allāh is truth and the Hour [is coming] - no doubt about it,' you said, 'We know not what is the Hour. We assume only assumption, and we are not convinced.'"
- THE AYAH: وَبَدَا لَهُمۡ سَيِّـَٔاتُ مَا عَمِلُواْ وَحَاقَ بِهِم مَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ | And the evil consequences of what they did will appear to them, and they will be enveloped by what they used to ridicule.
- After (45:34): And it will be said, "Today We will forget you as you forgot the meeting of this Day of yours, and your refuge is the Fire, and for you there are no helpers.
