# Similars brief: Surah 74 Al-Muddaththir

Each group lists every occurrence in muṣḥaf order. "DIFFERS" lists the words that differ from the first occurrence in this surah.

## g1: repeated within this surah · 2 occurrences

### 74:19: Al-Muddaththir, page 576, same words
- Before (74:18): Indeed, he thought and deliberated.
- THE AYAH: فَقُتِلَ كَيۡفَ قَدَّرَ | So may he be destroyed [for] how he deliberated.

### 74:20: Al-Muddaththir, page 576, words differ
