# Similars brief: Surah 43 Az-Zukhruf

Each group lists every occurrence in muṣḥaf order. "DIFFERS" lists the words that differ from the first occurrence in this surah.

## g1: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

### 43:2: Az-Zukhruf, page 489, same words
- Before (43:1): Ḥā, Meem.
- THE AYAH: وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ | By the clear Book,
- After (43:3): Indeed, We have made it an Arabic Qur’ān that you might understand.

### 44:2: Ad-Dukhan, page 496, same words
- Before (44:1): Ḥā, Meem.
- THE AYAH: وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ | By the clear Book,
- After (44:3): Indeed, We sent it down during a blessed night. Indeed, We were to warn [mankind].

## g2: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

### 12:2: Yusuf, page 235, words differ
- Before (12:1): Alif, Lām, Rā. These are the verses of the clear Book.
- THE AYAH: إِنَّآ أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ قُرۡءَٰنًا عَرَبِيّٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ | Indeed, We have sent it down as an Arabic Qur’ān that you might understand.
- DIFFERS: أَنزَلۡنَٰهُ
- After (12:3): We relate to you, [O Muḥammad], the best of stories in what We have revealed to you of this Qur’ān although you were, before it, among the unaware.

### 43:3: Az-Zukhruf, page 489, same words
- Before (43:2): By the clear Book,
- THE AYAH: إِنَّا جَعَلۡنَٰهُ قُرۡءَٰنًا عَرَبِيّٗا لَّعَلَّكُمۡ تَعۡقِلُونَ | Indeed, We have made it an Arabic Qur’ān that you might understand.
- After (43:4): And indeed it is, in the Mother of the Book with Us, exalted and full of wisdom.

## g3: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

### 15:11: Al-Hijr, page 262, words differ
- Before (15:10): And We had certainly sent [messengers] before you, [O Muḥammad], among the sects of the former peoples.
- THE AYAH: وَمَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن رَّسُولٍ إِلَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ | And no messenger would come to them except that they ridiculed him.
- DIFFERS: رَّسُولٍ
- After (15:12): Thus do We insert it [i.e., denial] into the hearts of the criminals.

### 43:7: Az-Zukhruf, page 489, same words
- Before (43:6): And how many a prophet We sent among the former peoples,
- THE AYAH: وَمَا يَأۡتِيهِم مِّن نَّبِيٍّ إِلَّا كَانُواْ بِهِۦ يَسۡتَهۡزِءُونَ | But there would not come to them a prophet except that they used to ridicule him.
- After (43:8): And We destroyed greater than them in [striking] power, and the example of the former peoples has preceded.

## g4: matches ayat in other surahs · 3 occurrences

### 3:51: Ali 'Imran, page 56, words differ
- Before (3:50): And [I have come] confirming what was before me of the Torah and to make lawful for you some of what was forbidden to you. And I have come to you with a sign from your Lord, so fear Allāh and obey me.
- THE AYAH: إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ رَبِّي وَرَبُّكُمۡ فَٱعۡبُدُوهُۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٞ مُّسۡتَقِيمٞ | Indeed, Allāh is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him. That is the straight path.'"
- After (3:52): But when Jesus felt [persistence in] disbelief from them, he said, "Who are my supporters for [the cause of] Allāh?" The disciples said, "We are supporters for Allāh. We have believed in Allāh and testify that we are Muslims [submitting to Him].

### 19:36: Maryam, page 307, words differ
- Before (19:35): It is not [befitting] for Allāh to take a son; exalted is He! When He decrees an affair, He only says to it, "Be," and it is.
- THE AYAH: وَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ رَبِّي وَرَبُّكُمۡ فَٱعۡبُدُوهُۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٞ مُّسۡتَقِيمٞ | [Jesus said], "And indeed, Allāh is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him. That is a straight path."
- DIFFERS: وَإِنَّ
- After (19:37): Then the factions differed [concerning Jesus] from among them, so woe to those who disbelieved - from the scene of a tremendous Day.

### 43:64: Az-Zukhruf, page 494, same words
- Before (43:63): And when Jesus brought clear proofs, he said, "I have come to you with wisdom [i.e., prophethood] and to make clear to you some of that over which you differ, so fear Allāh and obey me.
- THE AYAH: إِنَّ ٱللَّهَ هُوَ رَبِّي وَرَبُّكُمۡ فَٱعۡبُدُوهُۚ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطٞ مُّسۡتَقِيمٞ | Indeed, Allāh is my Lord and your Lord, so worship Him. This is a straight path."
- After (43:65): But the denominations from among them differed [and separated], so woe to those who have wronged from the punishment of a painful Day.

## g5: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

### 43:83: Az-Zukhruf, page 495, same words
- Before (43:82): Exalted is the Lord of the heavens and the earth, Lord of the Throne, above what they describe.
- THE AYAH: فَذَرۡهُمۡ يَخُوضُواْ وَيَلۡعَبُواْ حَتَّىٰ يُلَٰقُواْ يَوۡمَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي يُوعَدُونَ | So leave them to converse vainly and amuse themselves until they meet their Day which they are promised.
- After (43:84): And it is He [i.e., Allāh] who is [the only] deity in the heaven, and on the earth [the only] deity. And He is the Wise, the Knowing.

### 70:42: Al-Ma'arij, page 570, same words
- Before (70:41): To replace them with better than them; and We are not to be outdone.
- THE AYAH: فَذَرۡهُمۡ يَخُوضُواْ وَيَلۡعَبُواْ حَتَّىٰ يُلَٰقُواْ يَوۡمَهُمُ ٱلَّذِي يُوعَدُونَ | So leave them to converse vainly and amuse themselves until they meet their Day which they are promised -
- After (70:43): The Day they will emerge from the graves rapidly as if they were, toward an erected idol, hastening.
