# Similars brief: Surah 67 Al-Mulk

Each group lists every occurrence in muṣḥaf order. "DIFFERS" lists the words that differ from the first occurrence in this surah.

## g1: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

### 23:79: Al-Mu'minun, page 347, words differ
- Before (23:78): And it is He who produced for you hearing and vision and hearts [i.e., intellect]; little are you grateful.
- THE AYAH: وَهُوَ ٱلَّذِي ذَرَأَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ | And it is He who has multiplied you throughout the earth, and to Him you will be gathered.
- DIFFERS: وَهُوَ
- After (23:80): And it is He who gives life and causes death, and His is the alternation of the night and the day. Then will you not reason?

### 67:24: Al-Mulk, page 563, same words
- Before (67:23): Say, "It is He who has produced you and made for you hearing and vision and hearts [i.e., intellect]; little are you grateful."
- THE AYAH: قُلۡ هُوَ ٱلَّذِي ذَرَأَكُمۡ فِي ٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَإِلَيۡهِ تُحۡشَرُونَ | Say, "It is He who has multiplied you throughout the earth, and to Him you will be gathered."
- After (67:25): And they say, "When is this promise, if you should be truthful?"

## g2: matches ayat in other surahs · 7 occurrences

### 10:48: Yunus, page 214, same words
- Before (10:47): And for every nation is a messenger. So when their messenger comes, it will be judged between them in justice, and they will not be wronged.
- THE AYAH: وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ | And they say, "When is [the fulfillment of] this promise, if you should be truthful?"
- After (10:49): Say, "I possess not for myself any harm or benefit except what Allāh should will. For every nation is a [specified] term. When their time has come, then they will not remain behind an hour, nor will they precede [it]."

### 21:38: Al-Anbya, page 325, same words
- Before (21:37): Man was created of haste [i.e., impatience]. I will show you My signs [i.e., vengeance], so do not impatiently urge Me.
- THE AYAH: وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ | And they say, "When is this promise, if you should be truthful?"
- After (21:39): If those who disbelieved but knew the time when they will not avert the Fire from their faces or from their backs and they will not be aided...

### 27:71: An-Naml, page 383, same words
- Before (27:70): And grieve not over them or be in distress from what they conspire.
- THE AYAH: وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ | And they say, "When is [the fulfillment of] this promise, if you should be truthful?"
- After (27:72): Say, "Perhaps it is close behind you [i.e., very near] - some of that for which you are impatient.

### 32:28: As-Sajdah, page 417, words differ
- Before (32:27): Have they not seen that We drive water [in clouds] to barren land and bring forth thereby crops from which their livestock eat and [they] themselves? Then do they not see?
- THE AYAH: وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡفَتۡحُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ | And they say, "When will be this conquest, if you should be truthful?"
- DIFFERS: ٱلۡفَتۡحُ
- After (32:29): Say, [O Muḥammad], "On the Day of Conquest the belief of those who had disbelieved will not benefit them, nor will they be reprieved."

### 34:29: Saba, page 431, same words
- Before (34:28): And We have not sent you except comprehensively to mankind as a bringer of good tidings and a warner. But most of the people do not know.
- THE AYAH: وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ | And they say, "When is this promise, if you should be truthful?"
- After (34:30): Say, "For you is the appointment of a Day [when] you will not remain thereafter an hour, nor will you precede [it]."

### 36:48: Ya-Sin, page 443, same words
- Before (36:47): And when it is said to them, "Spend from that which Allāh has provided for you," those who disbelieve say to those who believe, "Should we feed one whom, if Allāh had willed, He would have fed? You are not but in clear error."
- THE AYAH: وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ | And they say, "When is this promise, if you should be truthful?"
- After (36:49): They do not await except one blast which will seize them while they are disputing.

### 67:25: Al-Mulk, page 563, same words
- Before (67:24): Say, "It is He who has multiplied you throughout the earth, and to Him you will be gathered."
- THE AYAH: وَيَقُولُونَ مَتَىٰ هَٰذَا ٱلۡوَعۡدُ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ | And they say, "When is this promise, if you should be truthful?"
- After (67:26): Say, "The knowledge is only with Allāh, and I am only a clear warner."
