# Similars brief: Surah 81 At-Takwir

Each group lists every occurrence in muṣḥaf order. "DIFFERS" lists the words that differ from the first occurrence in this surah.

## g1: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

### 69:40: Al-Haqqah, page 568, same words
- Before (69:39): And what you do not see
- THE AYAH: إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلُ رَسُولٖ كَرِيمٖ | [That] indeed, it [i.e., the Qur’ān] is the word of a noble Messenger.
- After (69:41): And it is not the word of a poet; little do you believe.

### 81:19: At-Takwir, page 586, same words
- Before (81:18): And by the dawn when it breathes [i.e., stirs]
- THE AYAH: إِنَّهُۥ لَقَوۡلُ رَسُولٖ كَرِيمٖ | [That] indeed, it [i.e., the Qur’ān] is a word [conveyed by] a noble messenger [i.e., Gabriel]
- After (81:20): [Who is] possessed of power and with the Owner of the Throne, secure [in position],

## g2: matches ayat in other surahs · 3 occurrences

### 38:87: Sad, page 458, same words
- Before (38:86): Say, [O Muḥammad], "I do not ask you for it [i.e., the Qur’ān] any payment, and I am not of the pretentious.
- THE AYAH: إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرٞ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ | It is but a reminder to the worlds.
- After (38:88): And you will surely know [the truth of] its information after a time."

### 68:52: Al-Qalam, page 566, words differ
- Before (68:51): And indeed, those who disbelieve would almost make you slip with their eyes [i.e., looks] when they hear the message, and they say, "Indeed, he is mad."
- THE AYAH: وَمَا هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرٞ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ | But it is not except a reminder to the worlds.
- DIFFERS: وَمَا
- After (69:1): The Inevitable Reality -

### 81:27: At-Takwir, page 586, same words
- Before (81:26): So where are you going?
- THE AYAH: إِنۡ هُوَ إِلَّا ذِكۡرٞ لِّلۡعَٰلَمِينَ | It is not except a reminder to the worlds
- After (81:28): For whoever wills among you to take a right course.
