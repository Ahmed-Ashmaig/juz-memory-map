# Similars brief: Surah 82 Al-Infitar

Each group lists every occurrence in muṣḥaf order. "DIFFERS" lists the words that differ from the first occurrence in this surah.

## g1: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

### 82:13: Al-Infitar, page 587, same words
- Before (82:12): They know whatever you do.
- THE AYAH: إِنَّ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارَ لَفِي نَعِيمٖ | Indeed, the righteous will be in pleasure,
- After (82:14): And indeed, the wicked will be in Hellfire.

### 83:22: Al-Mutaffifin, page 588, same words
- Before (83:21): Which is witnessed by those brought near [to Allāh].
- THE AYAH: إِنَّ ٱلۡأَبۡرَارَ لَفِي نَعِيمٍ | Indeed, the righteous will be in pleasure
- After (83:23): On adorned couches, observing.

## g2: repeated here and in other surahs · 3 occurrences

### 77:14: Al-Mursalat, page 580, words differ
- Before (77:13): For the Day of Judgement.
- THE AYAH: وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلۡفَصۡلِ | And what can make you know what is the Day of Judgement?
- DIFFERS: ٱلۡفَصۡلِ
- After (77:15): Woe, that Day, to the deniers.

### 82:17: Al-Infitar, page 587, same words
- Before (82:16): And never therefrom will they be absent.
- THE AYAH: وَمَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ | And what can make you know what is the Day of Recompense?
- After (82:18): Then, what can make you know what is the Day of Recompense?

### 82:18: Al-Infitar, page 587, words differ
- Before (82:17): And what can make you know what is the Day of Recompense?
- THE AYAH: ثُمَّ مَآ أَدۡرَىٰكَ مَا يَوۡمُ ٱلدِّينِ | Then, what can make you know what is the Day of Recompense?
- DIFFERS: ثُمَّ مَآ
- After (82:19): It is the Day when a soul will not possess for another soul [power to do] a thing; and the command, that Day, is [entirely] with Allāh.
