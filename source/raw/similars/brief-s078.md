# Similars brief: Surah 78 An-Naba

Each group lists every occurrence in muṣḥaf order. "DIFFERS" lists the words that differ from the first occurrence in this surah.

## g1: repeated within this surah · 2 occurrences

### 78:4: An-Naba, page 582, same words
- Before (78:3): That over which they are in disagreement.
- THE AYAH: كـَلَّا سَيَعۡلَمُونَ | No! They are going to know.
- After (78:5): Then, no! They are going to know.

### 78:5: An-Naba, page 582, words differ
- Before (78:4): No! They are going to know.
- THE AYAH: ثُمَّ كـَلَّا سَيَعۡلَمُونَ | Then, no! They are going to know.
- DIFFERS: ثُمَّ
- After (78:6): Have We not made the earth a resting place?
