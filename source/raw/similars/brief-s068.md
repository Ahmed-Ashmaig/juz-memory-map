# Similars brief: Surah 68 Al-Qalam

Each group lists every occurrence in muṣḥaf order. "DIFFERS" lists the words that differ from the first occurrence in this surah.

## g1: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

### 6:117: Al-An'am, page 142, words differ
- Before (6:116): And if you obey most of those upon the earth, they will mislead you from the way of Allāh. They follow not except assumption, and they are not but misjudging.
- THE AYAH: إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ مَن يَضِلُّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦۖ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ | Indeed, your Lord is most knowing of who strays from His way, and He is most knowing of the [rightly] guided.
- DIFFERS: مَن يَضِلُّ
- After (6:118): So eat of that [meat] upon which the name of Allāh has been mentioned, if you are believers in His verses [i.e., revealed law].

### 68:7: Al-Qalam, page 564, same words
- Before (68:6): Which of you is the afflicted [by a devil].
- THE AYAH: إِنَّ رَبَّكَ هُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِمَن ضَلَّ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ وَهُوَ أَعۡلَمُ بِٱلۡمُهۡتَدِينَ | Indeed, your Lord is most knowing of who has gone astray from His way, and He is most knowing of the [rightly] guided.
- After (68:8): Then do not obey the deniers.
