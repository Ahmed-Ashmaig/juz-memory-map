# Similars brief: Surah 50 Qaf

Each group lists every occurrence in muṣḥaf order. "DIFFERS" lists the words that differ from the first occurrence in this surah.

## g1: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

### 15:19: Al-Hijr, page 263, words differ
- Before (15:18): Except one who steals a hearing and is pursued by a clear burning flame.
- THE AYAH: وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ مَدَدۡنَٰهَا وَأَلۡقَيۡنَا فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ وَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ شَيۡءٖ مَّوۡزُونٖ | And the earth - We have spread it and cast therein firmly set mountains and caused to grow therein [something] of every well-balanced thing.
- DIFFERS: شَيۡءٖ مَّوۡزُونٖ
- After (15:20): And We have made for you therein means of living and [for] those for whom you are not providers.

### 50:7: Qaf, page 518, same words
- Before (50:6): Have they not looked at the heaven above them - how We structured it and adorned it and [how] it has no rifts?
- THE AYAH: وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ مَدَدۡنَٰهَا وَأَلۡقَيۡنَا فِيهَا رَوَٰسِيَ وَأَنۢبَتۡنَا فِيهَا مِن كُلِّ زَوۡجِۭ بَهِيجٖ | And the earth - We spread it out and cast therein firmly set mountains and made grow therein [something] of every beautiful kind,
- After (50:8): Giving insight and a reminder for every servant who turns [to Allāh].
