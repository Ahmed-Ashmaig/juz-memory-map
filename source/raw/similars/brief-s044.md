# Similars brief: Surah 44 Ad-Dukhan

Each group lists every occurrence in muṣḥaf order. "DIFFERS" lists the words that differ from the first occurrence in this surah.

## g1: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

### 43:2: Az-Zukhruf, page 489, same words
- Before (43:1): Ḥā, Meem.
- THE AYAH: وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ | By the clear Book,
- After (43:3): Indeed, We have made it an Arabic Qur’ān that you might understand.

### 44:2: Ad-Dukhan, page 496, same words
- Before (44:1): Ḥā, Meem.
- THE AYAH: وَٱلۡكِتَٰبِ ٱلۡمُبِينِ | By the clear Book,
- After (44:3): Indeed, We sent it down during a blessed night. Indeed, We were to warn [mankind].

## g2: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

### 26:24: Ash-Shu'ara, page 368, words differ
- Before (26:23): Said Pharaoh, "And what is the Lord of the worlds?"
- THE AYAH: قَالَ رَبُّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآۖ إِن كُنتُم مُّوقِنِينَ | [Moses] said, "The Lord of the heavens and earth and that between them, if you should be convinced."
- DIFFERS: قَالَ
- After (26:25): [Pharaoh] said to those around him, "Do you not hear?"

### 44:7: Ad-Dukhan, page 496, same words
- Before (44:6): As mercy from your Lord. Indeed, He is the Hearing, the Knowing,
- THE AYAH: رَبِّ ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضِ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَآۖ إِن كُنتُم مُّوقِنِينَ | Lord of the heavens and the earth and that between them, if you would be certain.
- After (44:8): There is no deity except Him; He gives life and causes death. [He is] your Lord and the Lord of your first forefathers.

## g3: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

### 37:157: As-Saffat, page 452, words differ
- Before (37:156): Or do you have a clear authority?
- THE AYAH: فَأۡتُواْ بِكِتَٰبِكُمۡ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ | Then produce your scripture, if you should be truthful.
- DIFFERS: بِكِتَٰبِكُمۡ
- After (37:158): And they have made [i.e., claimed] between Him and the jinn a lineage, but the jinn have already known that they [who made such claims] will be brought [to punishment].

### 44:36: Ad-Dukhan, page 497, same words
- Before (44:35): "There is not but our first death, and we will not be resurrected.
- THE AYAH: فَأۡتُواْ بِـَٔابَآئِنَآ إِن كُنتُمۡ صَٰدِقِينَ | Then bring [back] our forefathers, if you should be truthful."
- After (44:37): Are they better or the people of Tubbaʿ and those before them? We destroyed them, [for] indeed, they were criminals.

## g4: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

### 21:16: Al-Anbya, page 323, words differ
- Before (21:15): And that declaration of theirs did not cease until We made them [as] a harvest [mowed down], extinguished [like a fire].
- THE AYAH: وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَآءَ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا لَٰعِبِينَ | And We did not create the heaven and earth and that between them in play.
- DIFFERS: ٱلسَّمَآءَ
- After (21:17): Had We intended to take a diversion, We could have taken it from [what is] with Us - if [indeed] We were to do so.

### 44:38: Ad-Dukhan, page 497, same words
- Before (44:37): Are they better or the people of Tubbaʿ and those before them? We destroyed them, [for] indeed, they were criminals.
- THE AYAH: وَمَا خَلَقۡنَا ٱلسَّمَٰوَٰتِ وَٱلۡأَرۡضَ وَمَا بَيۡنَهُمَا لَٰعِبِينَ | And We did not create the heavens and earth and that between them in play.
- After (44:39): We did not create them except in truth, but most of them do not know.

## g5: matches ayat in other surahs · 2 occurrences

- Before (26:146): Will you be left in what is here, secure [from death],
- THE AYAH: فِي جَنَّٰتٖ وَعُيُونٖ | Within gardens and springs
- After (26:148): And fields of crops and palm trees with softened fruit?

### 44:52: Ad-Dukhan, page 498, same words
- Before (44:51): Indeed, the righteous will be in a secure place:
- THE AYAH: فِي جَنَّٰتٖ وَعُيُونٖ | Within gardens and springs,
- After (44:53): Wearing [garments of] fine silk and brocade, facing each other.
