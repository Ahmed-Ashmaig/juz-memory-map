# Similars brief: Surah 54 Al-Qamar

Each group lists every occurrence in muṣḥaf order. "DIFFERS" lists the words that differ from the first occurrence in this surah.

## g1: repeated within this surah · 4 occurrences

### 54:16: Al-Qamar, page 529, same words
- Before (54:15): And We left it as a sign, so is there any who will remember?
- THE AYAH: فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ | And how [severe] were My punishment and warning.
- After (54:17): And We have certainly made the Qur’ān easy for remembrance, so is there any who will remember?

### 54:18: Al-Qamar, page 529, words differ
- Before (54:17): And We have certainly made the Qur’ān easy for remembrance, so is there any who will remember?
- THE AYAH: كَذَّبَتۡ عَادٞ فَكَيۡفَ كَانَ عَذَابِي وَنُذُرِ | ʿAad denied; and how [severe] were My punishment and warning.
- DIFFERS: كَذَّبَتۡ عَادٞ
- After (54:19): Indeed, We sent upon them a screaming wind on a day of continuous misfortune,

## g3: matches ayat in other surahs · 5 occurrences

### 15:45: Al-Hijr, page 264, words differ
- Before (15:44): It has seven gates; for every gate is of them [i.e., Satan's followers] a portion designated."
- THE AYAH: إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّٰتٖ وَعُيُونٍ | Indeed, the righteous will be within gardens and springs,
- DIFFERS: وَعُيُونٍ
- After (15:46): [Having been told], "Enter it in peace, safe [and secure]."

### 51:15: Adh-Dhariyat, page 521, words differ
- Before (51:14): [And will be told], "Taste your torment. This is that for which you were impatient."
- THE AYAH: إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّٰتٖ وَعُيُونٍ | Indeed, the righteous will be among gardens and springs,
- DIFFERS: وَعُيُونٍ
- After (51:16): Accepting what their Lord has given them. Indeed, they were before that doers of good.

### 52:17: At-Tur, page 524, words differ
- Before (52:16): [Enter to] burn therein; then be patient or impatient - it is all the same for you. You are only being recompensed [for] what you used to do."
- THE AYAH: إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّٰتٖ وَنَعِيمٖ | Indeed, the righteous will be in gardens and pleasure,
- DIFFERS: وَنَعِيمٖ
- After (52:18): Enjoying what their Lord has given them, and their Lord protected them from the punishment of Hellfire.

### 54:54: Al-Qamar, page 531, same words
- Before (54:53): And every small and great [thing] is inscribed.
- THE AYAH: إِنَّ ٱلۡمُتَّقِينَ فِي جَنَّٰتٖ وَنَهَرٖ | Indeed, the righteous will be among gardens and rivers,
- After (54:55): In a seat of honor near a Sovereign, Perfect in Ability.

- DIFFERS: ظِلَٰلٖ وَعُيُونٖ
